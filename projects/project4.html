Pedra a Pedra — Museu Virtual da Calçada

A VR virtual museum dedicated to Portuguese calçada (traditional stone pavement) in the Algarve, built with a multidisciplinary team of designers, artists, and producers.

View on Itch.io

Overview

Pedra a Pedra is a virtual reality museum exploring the history, craftsmanship, and cultural value of Portuguese calçada — the traditional hand-laid stone pavement found across the Algarve. It walks the user through the journey of the stone, from raw material to its place in the street, using interviews, audiovisual recordings, images, and interactive 3D elements to make the experience immersive rather than just informational.

The project sat at the intersection of design, digital communication, audiovisual production, and 3D modelling, with a full multidisciplinary team behind it rather than being a solo or programmer-only build.

Technical Focus
